# Pipe Hitters

A browser run-and-gun prototype focused on smooth movement, aiming, and shooting. Block figures and a small test yard only — this is the core-mechanics pass.

Play: https://ma-name-is-jeff.github.io/pipe-hitters/

The default view is a fixed-yaw, semi top-down camera. First-person is optional from the pause menu or the V key. Clear the yard of bot soldiers to finish the mission.

## Controls

### Desktop
- **WASD** — move (screen-relative in top-down)
- **Mouse** — aim at the ground (top-down) or look (first-person, click to lock the pointer)
- **Left click** — shoot (in top-down the gun also auto-fires when an enemy is in your aim cone)
- **Shift** — sprint
- **Space** — vault a low obstacle (also happens automatically when you run into one)
- **C** or **Ctrl** — slide
- **R** — reload
- **Q** or **1 / 2** — swap Line Carbine and Hall Sweeper
- **V** — toggle top-down / first-person
- **Esc** — pause

### Mobile
- **Left stick** — move in the screen direction you push
- **Right stick** — aim; auto-fires when an enemy is in the cone and range
- **Vault / Slide / Swap** buttons
- Pause button in the top-right for camera, landscape, invert-stick, and sensitivity settings
